Last updated: May 13, 2026

KLA-MEET is a dating and social networking app based in Kampala, Uganda.

To provide matches, improve safety, prevent fraud, and improve our services.

3. Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We only share with service providers (hosting) and when required by law in Uganda.

KLA-MEET is strictly for users 18 years and older. We have zero tolerance for harassment, explicit content without consent, or minors.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account and data at any time in Settings > Delete Account or by emailing kla.meet.ug@gmail.com
